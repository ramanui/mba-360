About CAT 2025

The Common Admission Test (CAT) is India's premier national-level management entrance examination, conducted annually by one of the Indian Institutes of Management (IIMs) on a rotational basis. CAT 2025 is being conducted by IIM Kozhikode. First introduced in 1950s and in its current computer-based format since 2009, CAT is the gateway to India's 20 IIMs and over 1,200 other top management institutions across the country. CAT 2026 Preparation Strategy

August (Foundation): Cover QA basics — Arithmetic chapters (TSD, P&L, Percentages). Start VARC reading habit — 2 RC passages daily. DILR: 1 set daily from basic level.

September (Intermediate): Complete QA (Algebra, Geometry, Number System). VARC: Para jumbles, para summary daily practice. DILR: Mixed sets. Take 1 sectional mock per week.

October (Advanced + Mocks): Focus on weak topics only. Take 2 full mocks per week. Deep analysis of every mock — not just score, but error patterns. Attempt 20+ RC passages.

November (Revision + Mock Burst): 3 full mocks per week. No new topics. Revise formula sheets. Last week: light revision, sleep well, avoid lastminute panic. Day before CAT: No study.
