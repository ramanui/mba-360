About SPJIMR

SPJIMR is one of India's leading management institutes, known for its rigorous academics, industry-linked curriculum and a strong focus on values-based leadership. Since 1961 the institute has built a reputation for producing socially conscious, globally competent managers across its full-time and executive programmes.

The institute is consistently ranked among the top private business schools in the country and holds international accreditations from EQUIS, AACSB and AMBA.

SPJIMR is one of India's leading management institutes, known for its rigorous academics, industry-linked curriculum and a strong focus on values-based leadership. Since 1961 the institute has built a reputation for producing socially conscious, globally competent managers across its full-time and executive programmes.

The institute is consistently ranked among the top private business schools in the country and holds international accreditations from EQUIS, AACSB and AMBA.

Its Post Graduate Diploma in Management (PGDM) blends classroom rigour with immersive, practice-focused learning, while the institute's alumni network of 38,000+ spans leadership roles across industries worldwide. SPJIMR's 103-acre campus and low 7:1 student-to-faculty ratio support close mentorship throughout the programme.

Beyond the flagship PGDM, the institute runs doctoral, family business, entrepreneurship and executive education tracks, reflecting its broader mission of shaping responsible, enterprising leaders for India and the world.

SPJIMR's Post Graduate Diploma in Management (PGDM) is a two-year, full-time residential programme equivalent to an MBA. PGDM is approved by AICTE, accredited by NBA and AMBA UK, and consistently rates among India's top 10 management programmes. The programme offers a holistic approach to leadership development through a blend of classroom learning and curated immersive experiences, guided by SPJIMR's faculty of accomplished academicians and practitioners.

In the Financial Times Masters in Management (FT MiM) 2025 Ranking, SPJIMR was ranked 35th globally and 3rd in India. The institute secured 3rd place worldwide — and 1st in India — in the "Careers Service" category, and was also ranked #1 among Indian B-schools in Alumni Network, Value for Money, and ESG & Net Zero Teaching for its PGDM programme.

Bachelor's degree in any discipline with a minimum aggregate of 50%, along with a valid CAT / GMAT / XAT score. Shortlisted candidates go through a written ability test and a personal interview round.

Total programme fee is ₹24,86,000, payable in instalments. Need- and merit-based scholarships are available for eligible candidates, including special scholarships for women applicants.

Applications open in August, with a final submission deadline of November 30, 2025. Interviews are conducted between February and March, and the final admit list is released by April.

Common questions cover work-experience requirements, scholarship eligibility and hostel accommodation — reach out to admissions for a full FAQ list.

An internationally-oriented track combining SPJIMR coursework with partner-university modules abroad for a global business perspective.

A senior-level executive programme aimed at professionals with significant experience, preparing them for CXO-track leadership roles.
